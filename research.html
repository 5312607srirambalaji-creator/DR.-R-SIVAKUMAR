Application No: 202041054054

Inventors: Dr. R. Sivakumar, Snehasish Ghosh, S. S. Sridhar

Status: Published in Official Patent Journal

Inventors: Dr. R. Sivakumar, Snehasish Ghosh

Investigation Areas

Detailed Research Domains

Watershed & Hydrologic Modeling

Formulation of spatial decision support systems for catchment treatment, micro-watershed prioritization, and groundwater potentiality zonation.

Seismotectonics & Earthquake Hazard

Subsurface energy potentiality calculation, focus distance modeling, active fault monitoring, and infrastructure resilience in active tectonic regimes.

Disaster Mitigation & Vulnerability

Multi-hazard risk assessment, flood vulnerability analysis using bivariate statistics, and disaster risk reduction framework development.

Hyperspectral Remote Sensing

Narrow-band spectral reflectance analysis for water quality monitoring, inland reservoir limnology, and vegetation stress diagnostics.

Land Information Systems (LIS)

Cadastral GIS, land use and land cover change (LULC) trajectories, and wasteland reclamation frameworks supported by high-resolution imagery.

Urban Hydrology & Flood Risk

Modeling urban runoff regimes, impervious surface extraction, and coastal micro-watershed flood mitigation plans.
