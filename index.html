Overview

Bridging Earth Sciences, Geoinformatics & Seismic Hazard

Dr. R. Sivakumar holds a Ph.D. in Geoinformatics in Earth Sciences from Bharathidasan University (2001) focusing on Catchment Treatment Modeling, and an M.Tech. in Remote Sensing (1993).

His academic portfolio spans high-impact positions at Birla Institute of Technology (BIT Mesra), GIS Institute Noida, and SRMIST. As Principal Investigator, he has spearheaded major research initiatives funded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), NRDMS, and NRSA / ISRO.
